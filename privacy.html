Flickerkeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Flickerkeep is a private, single-user personal assistant. Only its owner signs in to it. This policy explains how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

  • Google Calendar: read-only access to the owner's events, and access to a separate calendar that Flickerkeep creates for its own entries. It does not change other calendars.
  • Gmail: read-only access to the owner's messages. Flickerkeep does not send, modify or delete email.
  • Google Drive: access only to files that Flickerkeep itself creates, used to keep the owner's backups.
  • Basic account information: the owner's email address, used to identify the connected account.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide features to the owner, such as answering the owner's questions about their schedule and messages, and keeping backups. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Storage

Data is stored on equipment controlled by the owner. Backups stored in the owner's Google Drive are encrypted before upload. Data is kept until the owner deletes it.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with anyone except as needed to answer the owner's own requests: when the owner asks a question, the relevant content may be sent to an AI model service the owner uses, solely to produce that answer. No data is shared with other parties, except where required by law.

Limited Use

Flickerkeep's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored data can then be deleted by the owner.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new date.
