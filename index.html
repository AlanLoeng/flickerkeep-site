Flickerkeep

Flickerkeep is a private, single-user personal assistant. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission, Flickerkeep can:

  • read the owner's Google Calendar events and add events to a separate calendar that Flickerkeep itself creates;
  • read the owner's Gmail messages (read-only; it does not send, change or delete mail);
  • store and read its own backup files in the owner's Google Drive (only files it created).

This information is used only to help the owner with their own schedule, messages and files.
